1. Purpose
Failover procedure for primary-db, the shared relational datastore used by payments-api, billing-worker, checkout-service, and auth-gateway.
2. Procedure
Promote the standby replica via the platform console, confirm replication lag was under 5 seconds at the time of promotion, and re-point dependent services' connection strings per their own service runbooks.
3. Escalation
Escalate to the SRE on-call for primary-db immediately on trigger.
Internal use only — Fenwick Cloud Engineering. This document is maintained by the owning team and reviewed on incident close-out or quarterly, whichever comes first. Report inaccuracies to the owning team's on-call channel.
