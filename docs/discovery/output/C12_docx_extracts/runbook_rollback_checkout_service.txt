1. Purpose
Covers the standard rollback and latency-triage procedure for checkout-service, the customer-facing checkout API that calls payments-api for authorization.
2. First steps on a latency report
Check whether payments-api or a downstream dependency (fraud-detection-worker, primary-db) is elevated first -- checkout-service latency reports are more often a downstream symptom than a checkout-service-local problem.
3. Rollback procedure
Run: fenctl rollback checkout-service --to-last-stable.
4. Escalation
Escalate to the Payments on-call engineer if latency has not returned to baseline within 10 minutes of ruling out a downstream cause.
Internal use only — Fenwick Cloud Engineering. This document is maintained by the owning team and reviewed on incident close-out or quarterly, whichever comes first. Report inaccuracies to the owning team's on-call channel.
