1. Summary
A deploy at 09:40 introduced a misconfigured connection-pool size, causing payments-api to exhaust database connections under normal traffic within 12 minutes, surfacing as elevated 5xx responses to callers.
2. Impact
Approximately 4% of payment authorization requests failed for 18 minutes; no customer funds were double-charged or lost.
3. Root cause
Rollback via fenctl restored the previous connection-pool configuration and resolved the issue within 6 minutes of rollback completion.
4. Root cause summary
Root cause category
Deploy misconfiguration.
