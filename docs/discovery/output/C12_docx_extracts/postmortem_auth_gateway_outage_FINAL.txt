postmortem_auth_gateway_outage v1.0 (DRAFT, 2026-03-10) -- the v1.0 preliminary hypothesis was disproven during investigation and is retained for audit history only.
1. Summary
auth-gateway rejected a growing share of otherwise-valid requests over a 90-minute window on 2026-03-14, escalated to SEV1 once downstream services began failing health checks.
2. Impact
auth-gateway rejected ~35% of requests for 90 minutes, degrading every service behind it. The exposed credential was rotated and repository scanning for embedded credentials was added to CI.
3. Root cause
Investigation traced the failure to a service-account credential that had been embedded directly in a configuration file committed to a shared repository. That credential was used to obtain broader access than intended, and the resulting anomalous access pattern triggered auth-gateway's internal protection logic, which began rejecting a growing share of legitimate traffic as a side effect. The v1.0 draft's traffic-spike/DDoS hypothesis was investigated and ruled out: request-rate telemetry for the window did not show the volume increase that hypothesis would require.
4. Root cause summary
Root cause category
Committed service-account credential enabling unintended lateral access.

Incident commander: on-call SRE. Communications: owning team lead. Follow-up owner: owning team engineering manager.
