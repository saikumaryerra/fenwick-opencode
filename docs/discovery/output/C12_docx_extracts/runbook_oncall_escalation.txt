1. When to escalate
Escalate any incident that a runbook's own escalation section points to, or any SEV1/SEV2 incident where the assigned on-call engineer has not acknowledged within 10 minutes.
2. How to escalate
File a ticket in the Ops Escalation queue, tagged with the incident severity and the affected service. Include a link to the relevant runbook and a one-line statement of current impact.
3. After escalation
The Ops Escalation queue pages the secondary on-call automatically for SEV1 tickets. SEV2/SEV3 tickets route to the team's normal queue.
Internal use only — Fenwick Cloud Engineering. This document is maintained by the owning team and reviewed on incident close-out or quarterly, whichever comes first. Report inaccuracies to the owning team's on-call channel.
