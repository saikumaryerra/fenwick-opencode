1. Summary
A batch of invoice settlements was submitted twice for 41 invoices after a network timeout caused the billing-worker's retry logic to resubmit the same settlement request.
2. Impact
41 invoices were double-charged; all were identified and refunded within 24 hours of detection.
3. Root cause
The retry was issued without an idempotency key, so the downstream payment processor treated the retried request as a new charge rather than recognizing it as a duplicate of the original attempt.
4. Root cause summary
Root cause category
Retry issued without an idempotency key on a network-timeout path.

Incident commander: on-call SRE. Communications: owning team lead. Follow-up owner: owning team engineering manager.
