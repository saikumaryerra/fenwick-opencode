1. Purpose
Rollback procedure for billing-worker, the async settlement/invoicing worker.
2. When to use this runbook
Use when a billing-worker deploy is suspected of causing settlement errors, duplicate charge submissions, or a spike in worker retry rate.
3. Rollback procedure
Run: fenctl rollback billing-worker --to-last-stable, then drain the retry queue manually before resuming normal processing -- billing-worker does not auto-drain on rollback.
4. Escalation
Escalate to the Billing on-call engineer if duplicate-charge symptoms persist after rollback and queue drain.
Internal use only — Fenwick Cloud Engineering. This document is maintained by the owning team and reviewed on incident close-out or quarterly, whichever comes first. Report inaccuracies to the owning team's on-call channel.
