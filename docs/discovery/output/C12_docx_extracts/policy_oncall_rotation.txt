1. Rotation cadence
Each active service has a weekly primary on-call engineer, drawn from the service's owning team. Deprecated services are wound down from the rotation once decommissioning begins; owning teams are responsible for removing a deprecated service's entries from the on-call tool.
2. Acknowledgement SLA
SEV1: acknowledge within 5 minutes. SEV2: acknowledge within 15 minutes. SEV3: acknowledge within one business day.
Internal use only — Fenwick Cloud Engineering. This document is maintained by the owning team and reviewed on incident close-out or quarterly, whichever comes first. Report inaccuracies to the owning team's on-call channel.
