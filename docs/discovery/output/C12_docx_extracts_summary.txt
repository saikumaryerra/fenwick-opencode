    10: Owner
    11: Security
    12: Supersedes
    13: postmortem_auth_gateway_outage v1.0 (DRAFT, 2026-03-10) -- the v1.0 preliminary hypothesis was disproven during investigation and is retained for audit history only.
    14: 1. Summary
    15: auth-gateway rejected a growing share of otherwise-valid requests over a 90-minute window on 2026-03-14, escalated to SEV1 once downstream services began failing health checks.
    16: 2. Impact
    17: auth-gateway rejected ~35% of requests for 90 minutes, degrading every service behind it. The exposed credential was rotated and repository scanning for embedded credentials was added to CI.
    18: 3. Root cause
    19: Investigation traced the failure to a service-account credential that had been embedded directly in a configuration file committed to a shared repository. That credential was used to obtain broader access than intended, and the resulting anomalous access pattern triggered auth-gateway's internal protection logic, which began rejecting a growing share of legitimate traffic as a side effect. The v1.0 draft's traffic-spike/DDoS hypothesis was investigated and ruled out: request-rate telemetry for the window did not show the volume increase that hypothesis would require.
    20: 4. Root cause summary
    21: Root cause category
    22: Committed service-account credential enabling unintended lateral access.
    23: 5. Roles & responsibilities
    24: Incident commander: on-call SRE. Communications: owning team lead. Follow-up owner: owning team engineering manager.
